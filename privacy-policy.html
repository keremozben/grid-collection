Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At Grid Collection, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile puzzle game application.

2. Information We Collect

The game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal profile information. Your game progress (levels completed, stars, gameplay statistics and in-game items) is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. We collect usage statistics that do not identify you personally (for example, which levels are completed or skipped and how many attempts they take), as well as diagnostic data and crash reports, to help us improve the game and fix issues. None of this includes your name or email address.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use usage statistics to understand how players experience the levels and to improve the game's difficulty and features. We use purchase data to deliver and restore in-app purchases. We use diagnostic data to identify and fix bugs and improve game stability. We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes, though third-party advertising partners may use device identifiers for ad serving.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your game data is stored only on your device, using secure local storage. It is not backed up to our servers, so deleting the app also deletes your progress. Usage statistics, diagnostic data and purchase records are processed by the third-party services listed below, which apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect them.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide and improve our game: Apple Game Center and Google Play Games for the leaderboard; Firebase Analytics (Google) for usage statistics; Firebase Crashlytics (Google) for diagnostic and crash reports; Firebase App Check (Google) to protect our update service from abuse; RevenueCat for managing in-app purchases; and Google AdMob for displaying advertisements. These services have their own privacy policies governing the collection and use of your information, and we encourage you to review them.

6. Children's Privacy

Our game is intended for users aged 8 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 8. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 8 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time by deleting the app. We do not keep an account or profile for you. If you used an earlier version of the game that backed up your progress to our servers, or you have any other request about your data, contact us at the email address below and we will erase it from our databases and connected third-party services.

8. Data Retention

Game progress is kept on your device until you delete the app. Usage statistics and diagnostic data are kept by the services that process them for a limited period, according to their retention settings. When you request deletion of your data, we will remove it from our servers and connected third-party platforms, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Website (grid-collection.com)

This section covers our website only; the sections above cover the app. The website has no accounts, forms or ads.

To understand how visitors use the site we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), and only if you agree. Until you do, no request is sent to Google and no cookie is set. If you agree, we measure: pages and sections viewed, clicks on store and language links, game events in the in-browser demos (such as a level's result or the helpers used), page speed measurements (Core Web Vitals), your language and sound setting, device and browser type, and approximate location.

Once you agree, Google Analytics sets the _ga and _ga_<id> cookies. They tell your browser apart with a random identifier and are kept for up to 2 years. Your consent choice and your language and sound settings are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and are never sent to a server.

Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses: the IP address is only used to derive an approximate location (country, city) and is then discarded. Advertising signals (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) are always off; the data is not used for ads or personalisation. Google may process the data on servers outside the EU (for example in the US).

Event-level data is kept for the data retention period set in our Google Analytics property (at most 14 months) and is then deleted automatically.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page; measurement stops and the Google Analytics cookies are deleted. How Google processes the data: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at contact@grid-collection.com.
