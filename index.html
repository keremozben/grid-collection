01 — The rule

One rule.
Eight directions.

Start on any cell. Every move jumps a fixed distance. The goal: land on every active cell exactly once.

Straight: skip 2, land on the 3rd

Up, down, left or right; over the two cells in between.

Diagonal: skip 1, land on the 2nd

On the four diagonals; over the single cell in between.

Whether the cells you jump over are filled or empty doesn't matter.
